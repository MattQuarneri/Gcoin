# **Gratitude Coin Spec v1.0**

## **A Human-Time Economic Model**

### **Abstract**

Gratitude Coin proposes a new class of currency whose issuance is tied directly to the finite lifetime of each living human being.

Unlike fiat currencies, which are created by governments and central banks, or commodity currencies, whose value derives from scarce physical resources, Gratitude Coin derives its issuance from something universally shared by every person:

**One finite human life, experienced one minute at a time.**

Every verified living individual receives one newly created Gratitude Coin for every minute they are alive—1,440 coins each day. These coins represent the individual’s present economic agency rather than accumulated wealth. Unspent daily coins expire at the end of each day, ensuring that the currency continuously reflects the present capacity of humanity rather than becoming a store of hoarded capital.

The result is a monetary system based on the continuous creation and exchange of human gratitude rather than debt or scarcity.

---

# **1\. Motivation**

Every economic system ultimately depends upon human beings.

Buildings have value only because people use them.

Money has value only because people agree to exchange it.

Technology has value only because it improves human lives.

Labor, creativity, compassion, education, invention, parenting, caregiving, and every other productive activity ultimately consume the finite time of living people.

Yet modern currencies measure these contributions indirectly.

Gratitude Coin proposes that the most fundamental economic resource is not gold, energy, or government authority, but **the finite lifetime of human beings.**

Every person possesses exactly one life.

Every life proceeds minute by minute.

Every minute spent helping another person is irrecoverable.

This makes time the most universally scarce resource in existence.

# **2\. Fundamental Principles**

## **Every Human Is Sovereign**

Governments may recognize citizenship.

Banks may recognize accounts.

Employers may recognize employment.

But prior to all institutions is the individual human being.

Gratitude Coin treats every verified living person as a sovereign economic participant.

Participation is not granted by wealth, employment, or nationality.

It derives from existence itself.

---

## **Time Is Finite**

Every human life contains a finite number of minutes.

Each minute spent cannot be recovered.

Unlike commodities, additional lifetime cannot be mined.

Unlike fiat currency, additional lifetime cannot be printed.

Time is naturally scarce.

---

## **Every Person Begins Equal**

Every verified living person receives:

**1,440 Mortal Minutes per day**

No one begins a day with more issuance than anyone else.

Economic differences arise only through voluntary exchange and the gratitude of others.

---

# **3\. Mortal Minutes**

The daily issuance is called a Mortal Minute.

One Mortal Minute represents one minute of a living person’s daily economic agency.

Each verified living individual receives:

1,440 Mortal Minutes every day.

Unused Mortal Minutes expire at the end of the day.

They cannot be hoarded.

Tomorrow’s issuance begins again at 1,440.

This creates a continuously renewing economic flow.

---

# **4\. Gratitude**

When one person spends Mortal Minutes, they disappear.

The recipient receives permanent Gratitude.

The transaction is therefore not a transfer of currency but a recognition of value created.

Conceptually:

Alice spends 30 Mortal Minutes.

Bob receives 30 Gratitude.

Alice’s daily capacity decreases.

Bob’s historical contribution increases.

This separates present economic agency from lifetime contribution.

---

# **5\. Reputation**

Accumulated Gratitude is not identical to reputation.

A reputation system should emerge from the structure of relationships rather than simply the number of coins received.

Factors may include:

* diversity of relationships  
* duration of relationships  
* reciprocal exchanges  
* independent verification  
* community trust  
* historical consistency

In this model, reputation becomes an emergent property of the human network rather than merely accumulated wealth.

---

# **6\. Identity**

The greatest challenge is ensuring that each living person receives only one daily issuance.

This requires Proof of Personhood.

Governments may provide one source of identity.

Banks, educational institutions, employers, social attestations, biometric systems, and cryptographic credentials may provide additional evidence.

Rather than relying upon one authority, Gratitude Coin envisions a decentralized trust graph in which multiple independent attestations increase confidence that an account represents one unique living human.

Privacy-preserving cryptography (zero-knowledge proofs) should allow a wallet to prove:

“I am one verified living person.”

without necessarily revealing:

“My legal identity is…”

This separates uniqueness from surveillance.

---

# **7\. Anonymous and Attributed Giving**

Most Gratitude should remain fungible.

However, individuals may voluntarily attach their identity to particular gifts.

These become signed Mortal Minutes.

A signed Mortal Minute represents:

“This minute of my finite life was intentionally given to you.”

Such minutes become unique historical artifacts.

Recipients may preserve them permanently.

Organizations may choose whether they accept anonymous, attributed, or collectible forms of Gratitude according to publicly declared policies.

# **Part II — Economic Transaction Model**

## **13\. Economic States**

Gratitude Coin recognizes that economic value exists in different states throughout its lifecycle.

Rather than treating all currency as identical, the protocol models value as progressing through a sequence of thermodynamic states.

All the dynamics we discuss below arise from one principled invariant and conservation law:

**The First Law of Human-Time Economics:** *Every Gratitude Coin originates from exactly one voluntarily spent Mortal Minute. Every Mortal Minute may mint Gratitude exactly once. After minting the living potential energy of time is converted to the decaying kinetic energy of time.*

### **State 1 — Potential (Mortal Minutes)**

Every verified living individual receives 1,440 Mortal Minutes (MM) each day.

These are newly issued units of economic potential.

A Mortal Minute represents one minute of future human agency that has not yet been exercised.

Properties:

* Issued continuously through daily existence.  
* Cannot be transferred between parties.  
* Cannot be accumulated beyond the current day.  
* Expires automatically if unused.  
* Always possesses maximum economic vitality.

Mortal Minutes are therefore not wealth.

They are present economic capacity.

---

### **State 2 — Realized Value (Gratitude Coins)**

When a Mortal Minute is voluntarily spent, it is permanently consumed.

At that moment, a new Gratitude Coin (GC) is minted for the recipient.

The recipient becomes the first owner of that Gratitude Coin.

The original Mortal Minute no longer exists.

This guarantees that each minute of human life may create economic value only once.

Formally:

MM (consumed)

↓

Mint

↓

GC (created)

The protocol therefore prevents double-spending because Mortal Minutes never circulate.

Only Gratitude Coins circulate.

---

### **State 3 — Signed Mortal Minutes**

When spending a Mortal Minute, the sender may optionally cryptographically sign the minting event.

The resulting Gratitude Coin permanently records:

* originating person  
* originating time  
* optional message  
* digital signature

These become collectible historical artifacts representing one identifiable minute of a person’s finite life.

Organizations may choose whether or not to accept Signed Mortal Minutes according to their published policies.

---

# **14\. Gratitude Coin Thermodynamics**

Unlike traditional currencies, Gratitude Coins possess an intrinsic vitality.

This vitality represents the degree to which the coin remains connected to active human economic participation.

Vitality is modeled as a continuous value between:

0.0 ≤ T ≤ 1.0

where

T \= 1.0 represents a newly created, fully energized coin.

---

## **Initial Vitality**

A newly minted Gratitude Coin begins with:

T \= 1.0

This reflects that it has just been created directly from a living person’s present economic agency.

---

## **Economic Half-Life**

If a Gratitude Coin remains inactive, its vitality naturally decreases.

The protocol defines an initial half-life of:

Six months

This means:

After six months:

T \= 0.50

After one year:

T \= 0.25

After eighteen months:

T \= 0.125

and so on.

Vitality therefore follows exponential decay.

The exact decay constant may be adjusted by future protocol governance as additional economic evidence becomes available.

---

# **15\. Economic Recharging**

Gratitude Coins regain vitality through continued participation in the economy.

Whenever a Gratitude Coin is transferred as part of a voluntary exchange, its vitality increases.

However, it never returns instantly to full vitality.

Instead it approaches full vitality asymptotically.

One possible recharge function is:

T\_new \= T\_old \+ r(1 − T\_old)

where:

T \= current vitality

r \= recharge coefficient

This ensures:

* active circulation preserves value,  
* dormant holdings gradually cool,  
* repeated exchanges maintain economic vitality,  
* perpetual creation of energy is impossible.

---

## **Adaptive Recharge**

The recharge coefficient need not remain constant.

It may instead depend upon overall economic participation.

Possible inputs include:

* active participants  
* transaction volume  
* unique trading relationships  
* geographic diversity  
* network utilization

As humanity becomes more economically engaged, the system naturally becomes more energetic.

Each wallet maintains two independent balances.

Daily Balance

Characteristics:

* regenerated every day  
* expires daily  
* cannot be transferred  
* highest vitality

---

## **Gratitude Coins**

Persistent Balance

Characteristics:

* transferable

* may circulate indefinitely  
* eventually decay if removed from economic participation

Wallet software should automatically display:

Nominal Balance

Total number of Gratitude Coins owned.

Effective Balance

The vitality-adjusted purchasing power of those Gratitude Coins.

Users should not normally manage individual coin vitality.

The protocol performs this automatically.

---

# **17\. Transaction Flow**

A typical transaction proceeds as follows.

Step 1

The payer authorizes payment.

The wallet determines whether payment should be satisfied using:

* available Mortal Minutes,  
* Gratitude Coins,  
* or an optimized combination.

Step 2

If Mortal Minutes are used:

those Mortal Minutes are permanently consumed.

New Gratitude Coins are minted for the recipient.

Step 3

If existing Gratitude Coins are transferred:

ownership changes.

Their vitality is partially restored according to the recharge function.

Step 4

The transaction is cryptographically signed and permanently recorded within the public ledger.

The protocol recognizes that many economic relationships are ongoing rather than isolated.

Examples include:

* salaries  
* mortgages  
* rent  
* pensions

Recurring exchanges naturally keep the Gratitude circulating within those relationships.

Consequently, long-term productive relationships maintain economic energy without requiring special institutional privileges.

The protocol therefore rewards continued contribution rather than simple accumulation.

# **19\. Economic Equilibrium**

The Gratitude economy operates as a continuous cycle.

Living People

↓

Daily issuance of Mortal Minutes

↓

Voluntary action

↓

Consumption of Mortal Minutes

↓

Minting of Gratitude

↓

Circulation

↓

Recharge through continued participation

↓

Cooling through inactivity

↓

Eventual economic expiration

↓

Replacement by newly created value from living humanity

Unlike fiat currencies, no central authority creates additional Gratitude.

Unlike commodity currencies, no scarce physical resource determines supply.

The only source of newly created economic value is the finite time voluntarily contributed by living people.

The economy therefore becomes a continuously renewing network of human participation rather than a permanently accumulating stock of wealth.
