There are **1440 minutes in a day.**  To the living these are irreplaceable **mortal minutes** and have inherent value to the person who gets them. Fiat currency tries to peg its value to fluctuating supply and demand curves. They depreciate in value (driving up prices) when there’s too much and inflate in value (driving up borrowing costs (aka interest)) when there are too few. Fiat currency has proved to be superior to material currencies like silver and gold because as the population grew it could be produced to fuel a growing economy.

Now, as we aim for a utopia where energy is free and the vast majority of laborious mental and physical work is done by robots we imagine a world of Universal Basic Income where people get an allowance to finance their lives. This might work. But because the currency we intend to use in this endeavor is associated with financial debts that have been modeled on a particular growth trajectory it might not. Technology is a powerful deflationary force. These financial models may not mathematically pan out based on their growth assumptions.
